# LOST Contact Photo Gate

Scope: 4 October baseline with 5/6 October dev follow-ups, BR-35/69 and FR-CHAT-01/02. This extends existing UC-034/040/041/042/044/045, UC-106/107 and UC-120; no new UC ID or full ownership-verification completion is claimed. Current catalogue reconciliation below is separate from the dated verification snapshots.

## Communication Workflow

1. An authenticated non-owner opens a direct-message draft for an active LOST post. No conversation, warehouse item or ownership decision is created by opening the form.
2. Upload one JPEG/PNG/WebP, maximum 5 MB. The server authorizes the target and reuses the existing Gemini/OCR image-analysis service and matching engine. Client-supplied scores/approval flags are ignored.
3. Compare server-observed title/description (55%), category (15%), visual tags (25%) and OCR (5%) against the LOST post's saved signals. Photo location/time are not trusted and receive zero weight. Existing mismatch penalties still apply. This is a hybrid descriptive similarity score, not a calibrated probability or raw image-embedding distance.
4. Current 6 October follow-up: similarity must be **at least 0.50**, including exactly 50%, without rounding a lower result upwards; analysis confidence remains at least 0.60. Provider failure, ambiguous analysis or a lower score leaves contact blocked. The browser retains the selected photo and message draft for retry.
5. A successful check is private, actor/post/revision-bound and valid for 30 minutes until attachment. The LOST composer immediately opens a real conversation after approval, without sending the typed draft; the checked photo appears as an IMAGE message backed by protected PHOTO evidence. A stable request key prevents duplicate rooms/photos on retries. Changed/expired/foreign checks are rejected. Ordinary FOUND drafts still open with the first message.
6. Existing non-owner LOST conversations without a consumed check remain read-authorized, but further messages and verification questions/answers cannot bypass the gate. They can attach a fresh approved check. The LOST owner and ordinary FOUND conversations are unaffected. Closure/refusal/reporting are not converted into ownership approval.
7. Only the Finder sees three boxed question suggestions derived from the uploaded photo analysis's item/visual features, without OCR data or expected answers. They come from the analysis response, carried in browser navigation state or the current room's photo-check state, not a new table/column. Ordinary room reads and approved-check replays do not synthesize category-template suggestions; a Lost owner/Claimant never sees this panel. Clicking sends plain text immediately, preserves any typed draft and collapses the panel to one toggle row, with focus returned to that toggle. It can be reopened; a failed send expands it again for retry. These are ordinary chat messages, not automatically answered verification assignments or an acceptance decision.
8. The owner receiving contact on a LOST post sees a persistent safety reminder below the chat header: request current item photos, compare private characteristics before arranging collection, and do not send money or sensitive information. It remains visible even when the contact photo gate has passed; similarity does not prove possession. The reminder is not shown to the non-owner contacting LOST or to either participant in a FOUND conversation. It uses the server's LOST-owner contact-photo state, not the legacy `foundPostId` field name, and changes no messaging/ownership gate.

## Ownership and Privacy Limits

Local 5 October custody follow-up: a consumed approved Finder photo also supports the explicit custody-request option under BR-53, without requiring a FOUND post. It does not prove possession or ownership. Physical intake and Staff ownership verification remain separate decisions, with unchanged dispute/hold/proof gates. This is an extension of existing UC-042/141-146, not a new actor goal or an accepted production rollout.

- A copied, edited or AI-generated item photo may still match. This gate does not establish live possession, identify the owner, prevent every extortion/no-show attempt or guarantee a genuine Finder. User-facing warnings discourage payments; reports and human verification remain necessary.
- `CONTACT_PHOTO_MATCHED` audit explicitly records `COMMUNICATION_ONLY`. Claim state remains CONVERSATION_OPEN, never automatically ACCEPTED. Actual custody intake, Staff identification, competing-case/dispute/legal-hold checks and private return proof remain unchanged.
- Approved images become participant-protected PHOTO evidence and a protected chat image. Both consented active participants may send additional private PHOTO evidence or chat images; the composer separates these actions. Evidence opens/focuses its upload form, while chat images have a composer preview and become IMAGE messages. Neither upload grants ownership rights. Raw private storage paths, raw image-analysis output and expected ownership answers are not returned by the check API or included in notification text. Unused drafts expire and maintenance cleans them; consumed evidence remains part of the authorized claim record.
- Post/claim/contact-photo media use authenticated Cloudinary with protected proxy and legacy local reads; unconfigured non-warehouse storage retains its existing private local fallback. Warehouse intake/return proof uses its own authenticated Cloudinary namespace and protected gateway; production cannot write local fallback or fall back on provider failure. The new custody CONTACT_PHOTO gateway reads the original claim namespace, not a duplicated upload or public LOST image. This does not claim that all legacy post/claim images were migrated; deployment/real-provider acceptance remains separate. See warehouse-media-rollout.md.

## Consistent Ownership and Read-Only Legacy Access

The LOST owner is the Claimant; the sender reporting the found item is the Finder, with or without a linked FOUND post. New participant rows use those roles. Legacy reversed labels are resolved from actual target ownership and participants, consistently in chat, Staff review, verified return and feedback; consent and earlier Finder history are not rewritten. The requester database key remains an idempotency key, not ownership proof.

Claim/verification/template GETs never approve pending legacy consent or create a room. An explicit authorized Finder decision is required. Source-post deletion is blocked atomically while custody or active cases/holds exist. Staff verification remains a separate in-person decision after physical intake; photo approval alone grants no return right. See BR-70/71 and the permanent [actor-journey regressions](../audits/full-system-audit-2026-10-04.md).

## Direct Room Verification

- Verification/item context prefers an explicitly linked physical FOUND source; when no source is linked, it uses the conversation's original LOST or FOUND post. A direct LOST room does not require inventing a FOUND post merely to read its category, question templates or verification state.
- The four Finder decisions remain role-restricted and are placed above chat messages. Reading verification or matching a contact photo never approves ownership. In a photo-approved LOST room the Finder can explicitly propose a meetup without minimum verification answers; its audit marks `communicationOnly: true` and warehouse verified-recipient queries exclude that proposal. Ordinary FOUND verification retains its answer requirement. The separate appointment-booking feature is not implemented by this proposal.
- Custody accepts an eligible Finder-owned FOUND, or the consumed Finder contact photo from that unlinked direct LOST room (at least 50%, both participants consented). The latter requires no FOUND creation/selection: physical post IDs stay NULL and custody/item records retain the exact claim/room/Finder relationship. The LOST post remains comparison context, not a physical FOUND source. Staff sees protected CONTACT_PHOTO provenance, takes separate mandatory receipt photos, and explicitly verifies ownership before a linked return; active cases, disputes and hold remain blocking. Generic post-based custody cannot forge this route. Existing quarantined LOST links are not repaired by this exception.
- Private item images and exact location belong to the selected post's actual owner, not automatically to the Finder role. The other participant receives only the permitted category, area and public image metadata.
- A failed verification read renders a red inline error and an explicit retry, while retaining chat messages and the typed draft. Automatic verification polling pauses after a failure; requests are coalesced and cancelled/checked against the selected room so a late response cannot replace another room's state.
- On 4 October, read-only probes of the affected shared LOST conversation confirmed successful verification reads for both Finder and Claimant, with the correct roles/category and no automatic ownership acceptance. No migration or shared data correction was required for this fix. Unit/repository, real authenticated isolated HTTP/SQL and desktop/mobile browser regressions cover these contracts.
